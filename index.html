About

Built around trust, clarity, and careful observation.

Hades works around the practical side of Web3: keeping community spaces manageable, helping users find their way, and paying close attention to how products behave in the real world.

The work often sits between people and systems. That can mean answering support questions, calming tense conversations, escalating issues with enough context, or writing down what happened so the next response is faster and cleaner.

On the security side, Hades is learning and practicing vulnerability discovery methodologies through research, careful testing, documentation, and responsible disclosure habits. The goal is not to look loud. It is to understand risk, explain it clearly, and keep improving.
